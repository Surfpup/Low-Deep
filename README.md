# Low Deep
This is the first time, by the way! My game, Low Deep, is a 2D Metroidvania that is still in its early stages, but I’ve added World 8! It’s free to play for now, too!

World 1-8 takes about 1 hours 30 minutes or more to complete, but it may take longer if you spend more time fighting the bosses. The bosses are Ashling, the Bone Warden, the Hive Queen, the Cinder Dragon, the Big Troll, Dragula, Queen Spider Web, 2 Hive Queen’s, and Gargoyle.

I just finished Dragula, and now I’m moving on to another boss, Queen Spider Web. We’ll see what the next boss is soon!

I have a hard time playing because I can only use one hand, but I’m still able to do it! I’m proud of what I’ve accomplished!
